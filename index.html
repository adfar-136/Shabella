Flaxseed Gel

For Hair & Face · 10% Flaxseed Extract

A lightweight flaxseed gel that works two ways. On hair, it's a leave-in styling gel that controls frizz and flyaways and adds soft shine, without the crunch. On skin, it's a soothing rinse-off mask that moisturises and helps brighten.

  • Controls frizz
  • Soft shine
  • Conditions hair
  • Moisturises skin
  • Brightens
  • Soothes
For hairApply a small amount to damp or dry hair, focus on mid-lengths to ends and style. No rinsing needed.
For faceApply a thin layer to cleansed skin, let it dry naturally, then rinse off.
